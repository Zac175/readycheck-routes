Readycheck Routes

Turn a Warcraft Logs report into a Readycheck raid route: every trash pull in the order you did it, how many of each mob died, and each boss kill. Works for Molten Core, Blackwing Lair, Ahn'Qiraj and Naxxramas. No sign-in needed - paste a public report link and go.

Options

2. Take it into the game

  1. Click Copy on the raid below.
  2. In game, open Readycheck (/rc), pick the raid, go to Route and click From Logs.
  3. Click Paste Route and press Ctrl+V.

This replaces that raid's route, but gear and reminders you set on your old steps are kept wherever they still fit.
